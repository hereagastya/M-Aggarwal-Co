## Direction contract

THESIS: Jaipur blue pottery as a CA firm: calm cobalt-on-white glaze, painted with restraint. Refuses the navy-gradient-plus-gold "finance hero" and boxed-card grids.

OWN-WORLD: glaze white ground (cool, never cream), cobalt carrying whole bands, turquoise as the single secondary, sky wash, cobalt-ink text. Exactly five tones (raise from Game Boy four-shade: strict tone count, no gradients). Hand-painted rosette and vine-rim motifs drawn in cobalt line; photos sit in round "plates" with a turquoise rim. Young Serif display, Figtree text.

STORY: a worried visitor sees calm, understands "CA in Jaipur, here since 2011, six services", sees the next statutory due dates, and taps Book or Call from a thumb-reach dock.
